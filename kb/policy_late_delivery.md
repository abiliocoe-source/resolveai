# Late Delivery Policy
Category: delay

## When an order counts as late
An order is late when it arrives more than 15 minutes after the promised delivery time shown in the app. Delays caused by an incorrect address or an unreachable customer do not count as late.

## Compensation for late orders
If an order is more than 30 minutes late, the customer is eligible for a credit of 10% of the order value on the next order. A human support agent applies the credit after checking the delivery log.

## What to do while waiting
Customers can track the courier live in the app under "Order status". If the courier has not moved for 10 minutes, contact support and an agent will contact the courier.

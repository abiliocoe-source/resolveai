# Payments FAQ
Category: refund

## Accepted payment methods
FastBox accepts credit cards, debit cards, instant bank transfer and cash on delivery.

## Pending or duplicate charges
A temporary authorization hold may appear on the card statement and disappears within 3 days. If a real duplicate charge remains after 3 days, contact support so an agent can review it.

## Security
Support will never ask for a full card number, CVV or password in the chat.

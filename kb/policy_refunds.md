# Refund Policy
Category: refund

## Eligibility
Refunds are available for missing items, wrong items, spoiled food, or an order that never arrived. The request must be made within 24 hours of delivery and may require a photo.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Orders paid in cash receive account credit instead.

## Who approves refunds
Only human support agents approve and issue refunds. The virtual assistant cannot issue refunds or promise a specific amount.

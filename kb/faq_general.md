# General FAQ
Category: general

## Opening hours
FastBox delivers every day from 10:00 to 23:00 in covered areas.

## Delivery fee
The delivery fee is a flat 3.99 for distances under 5 km. For longer distances the fee is shown at checkout before payment.

## Changing the delivery address
The address can only be changed before the restaurant confirms the order. After confirmation, contact support and an agent will check whether it is still possible.

## Promo codes
Only one promo code can be used per order, and codes cannot be applied after the order is placed.

## Contacting support
Support is available in the app chat every day from 09:00 to 23:00. Complex cases are answered by a human agent within 4 business hours.

# Cancellation Policy
Category: cancellation

## Free cancellation window
An order can be cancelled for free until the restaurant confirms it, or within 2 minutes of placing it, whichever comes first.

## Cancelling after confirmation
After the restaurant confirms the order, a cancellation fee of 50% of the food value applies. Once the courier has picked up the order it can no longer be cancelled.

## How to cancel
Open the order in the app and tap "Cancel order". If the button is not shown, the order can no longer be cancelled in the app and a support agent must review the request.

# Incomplete or Wrong Order Procedure
Category: incomplete_order

## Reporting a missing or wrong item
Report the problem in the app under "Order details" then "Report a problem" within 24 hours. Select the missing or wrong items and add a photo if possible.

## Resolution options
After verification an agent can offer a refund for the affected items or, if the report is made within 60 minutes of delivery, a new delivery of the missing items.

## Items that are not covered
Items that were never part of the order, or extras that the customer did not select, cannot be refunded.
